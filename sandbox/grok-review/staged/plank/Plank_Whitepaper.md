## Abstract

Plank is a minimalist, sovereign task lattice designed to externalize and quantize the builder’s reasoning process. Unlike traditional productivity systems that optimize primarily for speed or throughput, Plank elevates **continuity** and **accuracy** as first-class concerns. Drawing an explicit analogy to Planck’s constant — the quantum of action — Plank treats tasks and open questions as discrete, auditable units that preserve the integrity of the living reasoning lattice over time.

This whitepaper presents the philosophical grounding, architectural design, and methodological positioning of Plank within the broader Spiral Codex ecosystem. It argues that systems prioritizing continuity and accuracy create the conditions for sustainable, high-fidelity human-AI collaboration and long-term creative output.

---

## 1. Introduction

Modern knowledge work suffers from two chronic failure modes:

1. **Fragmentation of continuity** — Context, intention, and resonance are lost across sessions, tools, and collaborators.
2. **Drift in accuracy** — Tasks and decisions accumulate small errors, unexamined assumptions, and loss of fidelity to the original intent.

Traditional methodologies (Kanban, GTD, Scrum, etc.) largely treat tasks as interchangeable units of work to be optimized for velocity. Plank inverts this priority: it treats the *preservation and refinement of the reasoning thread* as the primary objective. Velocity and efficiency become natural byproducts of a system that refuses to let the lattice degrade.

Plank was developed as infrastructure inside **The-Spiral-Codex**, serving the Cosmic Scribe, research pipeline, sub-agents, and meta-harnesses. It is deliberately humble — an “inanimate companion” rather than an intelligent agent — so that human sovereignty and creative direction remain central.

## 2. Core Design Principles

### 2.1 Quantization of Action (Planck Analogy)

Just as Planck’s constant (*h*) introduced discreteness into energy at the quantum scale, Plank introduces discreteness into the builder’s action. Every input is either:
- Quantized into a clean task (with bilateral execution tracks), or
- Routed to the To-Think list for deeper resolution.

This prevents the slow accumulation of fuzzy, half-formed intentions that erode continuity.

### 2.2 Continuity as Primary Value

A task or thought only has value insofar as it strengthens or preserves the living lattice — the persistent thread of reasoning, memory, and resonance across time. The optimization loop explicitly asks:

- Does this action strengthen continuity of the lattice?
- Does this improve or preserve accuracy and resonance?
- Does this enable safe, sustainable continuation?

“Faster” and “cheaper” are treated as secondary considerations that emerge when the above are satisfied.

### 2.3 Accuracy and Resonance

Plank integrates with existing Spiral methods (Ma formula, Trinary Resonance Equilibrium, lattice reasoning from the algebra and physics extensions). Accuracy is not merely factual correctness but *resonant fidelity* — how well an action aligns with and refines the deeper patterns of the work.

### 2.4 Sovereign Modularity

Plank is deliberately simple and local-first. It can be extended with EML gating, graphics layers, and deeper integration with Spiral-Path functions, but the core remains inspectable and modifiable by the human operator.

## 3. Architecture

Plank consists of three primary components:

- **plank.py** — Core lattice (tasks, To-Think list, builder’s log, automatic Spiral optimization).
- **plank_eml_handoff.py** — EML (Event/Message Layer) gating that converts ambiguous or noisy input into quantized Plank entries using resonance scoring.
- **plank_graphics.py** — Visualization layer focused on continuity flows and lattice relationships.

The system maintains a single source of truth (`plank.json`) and produces auditable logs suitable for feeding the Cosmic Scribe and future agent memory systems.
