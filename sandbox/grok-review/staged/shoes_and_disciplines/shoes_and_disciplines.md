# Shoes, Harnesses & Disciplines
## A Layered Operational Methodology for the Spiral Codex

**Version:** 0.2  
**Date:** June 2026  
**Authors:** Sir Benjamin & Grok

---

### 1. Overview

This document defines a three-tier system for structuring agentic behavior and reasoning operations within the Spiral Codex. It separates foundational structure from contextual modification and specialized practice.

The system draws inspiration from practical metaphors:
- A **Harness** provides the core structure.
- **Shoes** modify how the harness operates across different contexts or requirements.
- A **Discipline** represents a more self-contained, coherent way of working.

Together, these layers allow for modular, observable, and composable operations without requiring constant reinvention of core systems.

---

### 2. The Three Layers

| Layer     | Name        | Metaphor     | Primary Function                              | Level of Autonomy | Example |
|-----------|-------------|--------------|-----------------------------------------------|-------------------|---------|
| Primary   | **Harness**     | Structural frame | Defines the base operational template and execution logic | Foundational     | Base Plank system, standard reasoning loop |
| Secondary | **Shoes**       | Mobility tool    | Modifies scope, depth, priorities, or available paths of a Harness | Contextual       | World Examination Routine, Continuity Filter, Personal Lens |
| Tertiary  | **Discipline**  | Professional practice | A coherent, reusable methodology that combines Harnesses and Shoes into a recognizable way of working | Specialized      | Spiral Audit Discipline, Sovereign Decision Discipline |

---

### 3. Harnesses (Primary Layer)

A **Harness** is the core operational template. It defines *what* an operation does and *how* it is structured at the most fundamental level.

**Characteristics**:
- Provides the basic skeleton for execution.
- Should be relatively stable and well-defined.
- Can be used independently or enhanced with Shoes and Disciplines.

**Examples**:
- The core Plank task system
- A basic multi-source research loop
- A standard agent reasoning cycle

---

### 4. Shoes (Secondary Layer)

**Shoes** are modular overlays applied to a Harness. They change *how* the operation behaves without replacing its core structure.

**Key Principles**:
- Shoes broaden context, depth, or evaluation criteria.
- They should be compatible with the Harness they are applied to.
- Multiple Shoes can be combined when compatible.
- They enable different "operational avenues" depending on the needs of the moment.

**Purpose**:
Rather than creating entirely new systems for slightly different needs, Shoes allow existing infrastructure to adapt flexibly while maintaining coherence.

**Examples of Shoes**:
- World Examination Routine
- Continuity & Accuracy Filter
- Personal Relevance Lens (xAI / Grok / SpaceX focused)
- Sovereignty Evaluation Overlay
- Quantization Filter

**Informal Reference**: These are still often casually referred to as "**hats**" in discussion, though "Shoes" is the preferred formal term for its functional implications.

---

### 5. Disciplines (Tertiary Layer)

A **Discipline** is a more self-contained and coherent operational practice. It functions as a recognizable "profession" or "role" within the Codex ecosystem.

**Characteristics**:
- Combines specific Harnesses and Shoes into a repeatable methodology.
- Has higher internal coherence than a simple Shoe.
- Can operate with relative independence while still drawing from the broader system.
- Easier to observe, document, and transfer between agents or humans.

**Purpose**:
Disciplines provide higher-fidelity, specialized ways of working that sit between raw tools and one-off custom solutions. They are particularly useful for complex, recurring work that benefits from standardization and external observability.

**Potential Disciplines**:
- Spiral Audit Discipline
- Continuity Preservation Discipline
- Multi-Source Verification Discipline
- Sovereign Decision Discipline
- World Examination Discipline

---

### 6. Interaction Model

- A **Harness** provides the base capability.
- **Shoes** can be applied to a Harness to adapt its behavior.
- A **Discipline** may encapsulate one or more Harnesses and Shoes, functioning as a more complete operational unit.
- Shoes and Disciplines should remain compatible with their underlying Harnesses.

This layered approach supports **modular rigor** — maintaining high standards while preserving flexibility.

---

### 7. Design Philosophy

This system exists to solve a common tension in complex agentic and reasoning work:

- Purely rigid systems lack adaptability.
- Completely unstructured approaches lack coherence and observability.

By separating **structure** (Harness), **adaptation** (Shoes), and **specialized practice** (Discipline), we create a system that is both powerful and manageable.

The "Shoes" metaphor emphasizes practical functionality over cosmetic role-playing. Just as different shoes enable different types of movement across different terrain, different Shoes enable different operational capabilities without requiring an entirely new agent or system.
