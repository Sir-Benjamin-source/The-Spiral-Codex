Linguistic Foundation In English grammar: “that” introduces restrictive clauses essential to meaning (removal alters core identity). “which” introduces non-restrictive clauses providing supplementary context (removal reduces richness but preserves essence). This binary maps directly to associative reasoning: “that”-type branches: load-bearing, defining, low tolerance for volatility. “which”-type branches: exploratory, enriching, higher tolerance but prone to noise. 3.
