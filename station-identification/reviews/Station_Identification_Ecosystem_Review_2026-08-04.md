## 3. Living Context from Prior Session — Parallel Differential Examination Form

The following parallel differential pair was composed in a prior working session and is recorded here as living context for future examination routines. It is **not yet implemented**; it is preserved as a candidate skeleton.

### Shared skeleton
- \( S \) = subject isolation strength (clarity of the central claim)  
- \( G \) = generality expansion (breadth and coherence of surrounding associations)  
- \( E \) = etymological descent (depth and scientific-Latin alignment)  

All normalised to \([0,1]\).

### Parallel differential pair

**Actionable claim trajectory** (constructive / forward):  
\[
\frac{dA}{dt} = S \cdot G \cdot E - \lambda_A (1 - C)
\]

**Paradox containment trajectory** (holding / contrasting):  
\[
\frac{dP}{dt} = S \cdot (1 - G) \cdot E + \lambda_P (1 - C)
\]

where  
- \( C \) = instantaneous coherence between generated associations and the subject,  
- \( \lambda_A, \lambda_P > 0 \) are dual damping / amplification rates that sum to a constant (system remains bounded).

### Reading the complementarity
- Both equations share \( S \) and \( E \).  
- They invert the role of generality \( G \).  
- The coherence term \( C \) appears with opposite sign.  

The pair behaves as complementary colours: one trajectory extracts and advances the actionable claim; the other identifies, contains, and preserves the paradoxical residue.  
Their sum \( A + P \) remains informative; their difference \( A - P \) measures how cleanly the filter has separated the relevant from the inconsequential.

This form can be discretised into Head-to-Head operators or left continuous for readiness-field style dynamics already present in the continuous bridges (qsc-stabilization).
