## The Protocol: Step-by-Step Intake of External Spores for Cosmic Scribe

This is the living procedure the Scribe follows (or delegates with oversight) whenever new testing materials arrive online or via human direction. It ensures nothing enters the canon/ testing resource or shared works without passing our gates and without an explicit G_exp measurement of the friendship in the transformation.

1. **Gather & Provenance the Source**  
   Record exact origin (URL, report title, date e.g. "Stanford HAI 2026 AI Index — Technical Performance & Responsible AI", Vectara leaderboard updated May 11 2026, suprmind.ai June 2026). Note any cross-verification. Apply initial E_shield (no obvious contradictions with prior canon/ entries).  
   *Art note*: Treat the report as a spore packet — not enemy, not oracle, but potential nutrient.

2. **E_Shield + Grandmas-Wisdom Proxy on Key Claims**  
   Surface the strongest claims (e.g. "error rates up to 42%", "Gemini Deep Think IMO gold yet top clock ~50%", "agents 66% OSWorld but 1 in 3 structured fails", "hallucination 22-94% on memory factual vs low on summary", "two models together can perform worse"). Run grandmas-wisdom proxy (or real skill) for Bullshit Meter 1-10. Target ≥7-8 for any number we will later compare against in framework or harness. Flag longitudinal notes (e.g. "check against next Vectara refresh").

3. **Excerpt + Run the Cosmic Scribe Test Harness**  
   Extract a focused, self-contained paragraph or two (the "spore excerpt"). Call run_cosmic_scribe_test (or equivalent in full env) with:  
   - theory_name = descriptive (e.g. "Stanford 2026 Jagged Frontier and Agent Reliability Spore")  
   - cs_concepts = explicit list (benchmark reliability, jagged frontier, agent task success, hallucination variance memory vs summary, multi-agent synergy failure)  
   - citation_dois = [] initially (or known ones from our canon)  
   - grok_assist=True (delegate symbolic/helical resonance and cross-theory mapping to Grok/Helix)  
   Record the full audit: coherency (PIE fidelity, convergence, bullshit proxy, recommendation), applicability (citation_validity, domain, fitness), overall_gate, grok_collaboration note.  
   *Example outcome (June 2026 run on the jagged spore)*: Coherency False (PIE fidelity 0.7313, coherence 0.6855), Applicability False (citation_validity 0.4 due to no DOIs in raw test, fitness 0.402), Overall FAIL — "KEEP IN SANDBOX - deepen grounding". Grok collab note: high alignment with PIE for ambiguity and DAER for gating. This FAIL is a *success* of the protocol — it correctly refused an uncited external spore.

4. **Deepen & Re-Gate (If Needed)**  
   Add citations from our existing canon/external/ (the 2026 update file, prior Vectara entries, etc.). Re-run or conceptually re-apply baselines. Note delta (e.g. citation_validity rises to 0.85+ once DOIs/links from our gather are attached). Only then consider the spore "authenticated for comparison use."

5. **G_exp the Friendship Act of Intake & Authentication**

   Result level (amplified/measured/soft/hold) and the numeric G_exp. Log it.
